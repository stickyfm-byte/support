TOPIC 01

Getting started

  1. Create an account using an email you can access, or use Discord if enabled.
  2. Verify your email with the newest confirmation message.
  3. Sign in and open the support portal.
  4. Choose a category, write a clear subject, and explain the issue.
  5. Return to the same ticket to read and send replies.

TOPIC 02

Account help

  • Check spam/junk for account emails.
  • Request a fresh reset link if the old one expired.
  • Never share passwords, authentication codes, or recovery links.
  • If sign-in still fails, contact the team through official community channels.

TOPIC 03

Gameplay issues & bug reports

Include what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, any error text, and the approximate time. Add a screenshot when useful. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information.

TOPIC 04

Reports & appeals

Keep reports factual and respectful. Include relevant usernames, approximate times, and evidence you are allowed to share. For appeals, explain the situation clearly. Please reply to an existing ticket instead of opening duplicates.
