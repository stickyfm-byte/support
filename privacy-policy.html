SITE POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This starter notice describes information that may be processed when using Mirage Support. The site operator must verify that it matches the actual service configuration.

Information we may process

Email address, account identifier and username; ticket subjects, categories, messages and timestamps; attachments uploaded by you; and technical or security logs generated by service providers.

How information is used

To authenticate accounts, answer support requests, preserve ticket history, investigate bugs or abuse, and maintain service security.

Ticket privacy

Ticket conversations are intended to be visible to the ticket creator and authorised support staff. Do not include passwords, recovery codes, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Service providers

The site uses static hosting and Supabase for authentication and database services, and may use Supabase storage for attachments. These providers process information under their own terms and policies.

Retention and security

Information may be retained as needed to provide support, maintain records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact the site operator to ask about access, correction or deletion, subject to identity checks, technical limitations and applicable law.

Updates and contact

This policy may change as the service changes. Contact the operator through official MirageSMP support channels for privacy questions.

This is a starter policy, not legal advice. Before relying on it, the operator should add verified controller/contact details, lawful bases, retention periods, international-transfer details and applicable user rights.
