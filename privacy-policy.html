SUPPORT PORTAL POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Mirage Support · Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy applies to support.miragesmp.org.

1. Scope and responsibility

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit or use Mirage Support, including when you create an account, sign in, submit or respond to a support ticket, upload an attachment, or contact the Mirage Support team.

Mirage Support is a support portal for the MirageSMP community. This policy applies to the support portal only. The main website at miragesmp.org, the Minecraft server, Discord servers, and other services may have separate privacy practices and policies.

For privacy enquiries, contact the Mirage Support administration through support.miragesmp.org and identify your request as a privacy enquiry. The administration signature is Iconology.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use the portal and which features are enabled, the information processed may include:

  • Account information: account identifiers, email address, username, authentication status and related account records.
  • Discord sign-in information: identifiers and profile information made available to the portal by Discord when you choose Discord authentication.
  • Support content: ticket subjects, categories, messages, replies, status changes, timestamps, and communications with support staff.
  • Attachments: files or evidence you choose to submit as part of a ticket.
  • Operational and security information: technical records generated by the website, authentication system, hosting provider, database, or email service, such as event timestamps and diagnostic or abuse-prevention records.
  • Privacy preferences: the cookie or privacy choices saved by your browser.

Please do not submit passwords, authentication codes, recovery links, payment details, or sensitive personal information that is not necessary to resolve your request.

3. How we use information

Information is used, as applicable, to:

  • create and authenticate support accounts;
  • receive, manage, answer, investigate and retain support tickets;
  • review reports, appeals, technical issues and suspected misuse;
  • send account-related or support-related emails where those features are enabled;
  • maintain the availability, security and integrity of the portal;
  • enforce the Terms of Service and protect users, staff and the service; and
  • respond to privacy requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal grounds for processing

Where data protection law requires a lawful basis, the basis depends on the purpose and circumstances of the processing. This may include taking steps at your request to provide the support service, pursuing legitimate interests in operating and securing the portal and handling support requests, complying with legal obligations, or relying on consent where consent is required or has been requested.

Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal or processing that can lawfully continue on another basis. The relevant basis and any additional information will be explained where required for the particular activity.

5. Tickets and access by staff

Ticket content is intended to be available to the person who submitted the ticket and authorised support staff with permission to handle it. Administrators may have broader access for legitimate support, moderation, security and operational purposes.

Staff are expected to use access only for legitimate service purposes. Do not assume that a ticket is visible only to the individual staff member who replies to it. Information may be reviewed when necessary to investigate a report, resolve a request, protect the service, or address misuse.

6. Service providers and sharing

Mirage Support uses third-party providers to operate the portal. Depending on the feature and configuration, these may include:

  • Supabase: authentication, database storage and file storage where enabled.
  • Resend: delivery of account-related or support-related email where configured.
  • Discord: authentication and the provision of information needed to complete Discord sign-in, if you choose that option.
  • Website hosting and delivery providers: hosting and delivering the portal and maintaining its technical operation.

Information is shared with providers only as relevant to the operation of the service, its security, and applicable legal requirements. Each provider may process information under its own terms, privacy documentation and agreements.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to address security incidents, investigate misuse, protect rights or safety, respond to lawful requests, or comply with legal obligations. It is not sold to advertisers.

7. International processing

Some service providers may process or store information in countries outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, the transfer arrangements and safeguards depend on the provider, the services enabled, and the applicable configuration. Appropriate safeguards will be used where required by data protection law.

You can consult the privacy and data-transfer information published by the relevant service providers for further details about their processing locations and safeguards.

8. Retention

Information is retained for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, taking account of the nature of the information, the status and history of a support request, operational and security needs, dispute handling, and applicable legal obligations.

Ticket records may need to be retained after a ticket is closed so the team can review prior decisions, respond to follow-up requests, prevent abuse, or maintain appropriate service records. Backups and provider logs may persist for a limited period under the relevant systems' retention cycles.

When information is no longer needed, it will be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements and the service's technical capabilities.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. These measures may include access controls, authentication, restricted database permissions and security monitoring where configured.

No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. You should protect your account credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorised access through the support portal.

10. Cookies, local storage and privacy preferences

The portal may use browser storage and similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember privacy preferences, and maintain relevant application state. Essential storage is used for core features such as authentication and cannot be disabled through the site's preference panel without affecting functionality.

The portal's privacy panel includes an optional analytics preference for future use. At the time of this policy's last update, enabling that preference does not itself activate an analytics service. If optional analytics or other non-essential technologies are introduced, the portal's implementation and notices should be updated, and any consent required by law will be obtained before those technologies are used.

You can revisit the available choices using the “Cookie settings” control in the website footer, where displayed. You can also manage browser storage through your browser settings; removing storage may sign you out or reset saved preferences.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to processing, or transfer of information. Where processing is based on consent, you may also withdraw consent.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal conditions or exemptions. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may retain certain information where there is a lawful reason to do so.

To make a request, contact the Mirage Support administration through the support portal and clearly describe the information or right involved. You may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Information is available at ico.org.uk.

12. Children and young people

Mirage Support may be used by members of the MirageSMP community who are under 18. Users should provide only information necessary for their request and should not share sensitive details about themselves or others unless genuinely needed. Reports involving another person should avoid unnecessary disclosure of private information.

13. Third-party websites

The portal may link to Discord, the main Mirage website, or other external websites. Those services are operated separately and are governed by their own privacy policies. Review their policies before providing information directly to them.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the portal, its providers, features, or legal requirements. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the portal or another appropriate channel where required.

15. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests relating to privacy and personal information, contact the Mirage Support administration through support.miragesmp.org.

Administration signature: Iconology
