MIRAGE SUPPORT CENTRE · HELP & ANSWERS

Answers before you need to ask.

Browse practical answers about your account, support requests, attachments, gameplay issues, player reports and appeals. If you cannot find what you need, open a ticket and give our team the context needed to help.

Accounts & sign-in

How do I create a support account?

Open the support portal and choose Create account. Enter a username, a valid email address and a strong password, then follow the verification instructions if prompted. Use an email inbox you can access, because it may be needed for confirmation and account recovery.

I did not receive my verification email. What should I check?

First check spam, junk, promotions and other filtered folders. Confirm that you entered the correct email address and give delivery a little time. If nothing arrives, try the latest verification option available in the portal or ask for help in the official MirageSMP Discord. Email delivery can be temporarily affected by provider configuration.

My verification link says it is invalid or expired.

Request a fresh email and use the newest link only. Links can expire or stop working after they have been used. Open the link in the same browser where you intend to use the portal, and avoid sharing it with anyone.

I forgot my password. Can support reset it for me?

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page and follow the email instructions. Check spam if the email does not arrive. Staff should never need your current password, one-time code or recovery link to help with an issue.

Can I change the email address on my account?

If account settings do not provide an email-change option, create a ticket from the email currently linked to your account and explain what you need changed. Staff may ask reasonable questions to confirm ownership, but never send passwords or authentication codes.

Is it safe to use Discord sign-in?

Use the official portal and check that the sign-in page belongs to the expected provider before approving access. Never enter your Discord password into a support form or send authentication codes to staff. If a sign-in window looks suspicious, close it and navigate to the portal directly.

Tickets & replies

How do I open a useful support ticket?

Sign in, select the closest category, write a specific subject and describe the issue in the message. Include when it happened, what you were trying to do, what you expected and what actually happened. Submit once and continue the conversation in that ticket so the history stays together.

Which category should I choose?

Choose Account for sign-in or account-access problems, In-game issue for gameplay problems, Bug report for reproducible faults, Player report for rule concerns, and Appeal if you are asking for a punishment to be reviewed. Use General Support or Other if nothing fits; staff can redirect it if needed.

What happens after I submit a ticket?

Your ticket should appear in your ticket list. Open it to read the conversation and send follow-up messages. Staff review requests as availability allows; submitting multiple tickets for the same issue usually makes the history harder to follow rather than speeding up a response.

How can I add more information after submitting?

Open the existing ticket and send a reply with the extra details. Keep updates in the same conversation, mention anything that has changed and attach new evidence if it helps. This gives staff one clear timeline to work from.

Why can I not send a reply?

Check that you are signed into the account that owns the ticket and that the ticket has not been closed. If it remains unavailable, refresh the page and sign in again. If the issue persists, contact the team through the official Discord and include the ticket subject and any visible error text.

What do ticket statuses mean?

Open means the request is awaiting attention or further handling. In progress indicates that it is being worked on. Closed means the conversation is marked as finished; if the issue returns, follow the portal’s available options or open a new ticket referencing the earlier one.

How long will a response take?

Response times vary with staff availability, the type of issue and the amount of information provided. We cannot guarantee an exact timeframe. Please avoid repeatedly opening duplicate tickets or pinging individual staff; add relevant updates to the original request instead.

What if the ticket list is blank or shows an error?

Confirm that you are signed in, then refresh the page. If the problem continues, note the exact error and whether it happened while loading tickets, creating a ticket, sending a reply or uploading an image. Send that information to support so the team can investigate the right part of the system.

Evidence, bugs & gameplay

Can I attach screenshots?

Where uploads are enabled, the portal accepts PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP and GIF images up to 5 MB per attachment. Use a clear image that shows the relevant information. If an upload fails, send the written report first and explain that the attachment did not work.

What should a bug report include?

Describe the steps that reproduce the problem, the expected result, the actual result, the approximate time it happened and any relevant server or game context. Include the exact error text and a screenshot when useful. Tell us whether it happens every time or only occasionally.

My game is lagging or I cannot connect. What should I do?

Check the official server announcements for outages or maintenance, confirm your connection is stable and make sure you are using the supported game version. If the issue persists, report when it started, the error displayed and whether other servers or services work normally. Do not post private account details publicly.

How do I report a player?

Choose Player report and provide the player name, approximate date/time, what happened and any available evidence. Keep the description factual and concise. Do not edit evidence in a misleading way, publish private information or use a report to harass another player.

Should I send evidence through a public channel?

Use the support ticket for information that should only be seen by the support team. Avoid sharing personal details, private messages unrelated to the report, account credentials or other sensitive information in public channels.

Reports, appeals & safety

How do I appeal a punishment?

Select Appeal and explain which punishment you are appealing, what you believe staff should review and any relevant context. Be respectful and honest. An appeal is a request for review, not a guarantee that the original decision will change.

What makes an appeal easier to review?

Include your in-game username, the approximate date of the punishment, the reason shown to you and a clear explanation. If you have new relevant evidence, describe it or attach it where supported. Avoid insults, repeated duplicate appeals or claims you cannot support.

Can I submit someone else’s account details?

Only include information needed to explain the issue. Do not submit passwords, login codes, recovery links, payment card details or unrelated personal information. If you are helping another player, let them contact support directly when account ownership needs to be verified.

How do I know a support message is genuine?

Start from the official support portal or the server’s official Discord. Be cautious of unsolicited messages asking you to install software, share codes, disclose a password or visit unfamiliar links. Staff should not need your password to troubleshoot a ticket.
