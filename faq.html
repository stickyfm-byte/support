Frequently asked questions

Common account and ticket questions.

Get support ↗
How do I create a ticket?

Sign in, choose a category, add a clear subject and describe the issue. Submit once, then continue in that ticket.

Where is my verification email?

Check spam/junk and make sure the address is correct. If it still does not arrive, try later or contact the team through the official Discord.

My confirmation link expired. What should I do?

Request a new link and open the newest email. Older links may expire or become invalid after use.

What should I include in a bug report?

Steps to reproduce, expected behaviour, actual behaviour, error text, and a screenshot if helpful.

Can I attach evidence?

Image attachments support PNG, JPG, WEBP or GIF up to 5 MB when uploads are configured.

How do I read staff replies?

Sign in and select the ticket from your list. Reply in the same conversation to keep the context together.

Can support ask for my password?

No. Never share passwords, login codes, or recovery links with anyone.
